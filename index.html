The Purity Guarantee

Why Our Kitchen Is Different

Industrial spices rely on heat machines, cheap starches, and chemical preservatives to extend shelf life. We strictly refuse short-cuts.

01. 100% Clean

Preservative & Color-Free

Never mixed with artificial red Sudan dye, MSG, fillers, or chemical anti-caking agents. The vibrant colors come purely from native Salem turmeric and sunny Guntur chillies.

02. Mara Chekku

Wood-Pressed & Stone Ground

Crushed using traditional slow-speed wooden expellers (Vagai wood mortar). Operating strictly under 38°C preserves original nutty aroma, high antioxidants, and natural vitamins.

03. Traditional Care

Hand-Cleaned Sun-Dried Spices

Every whole spice is hand-inspected to remove stems and grit, washed, and sun-dried for multiple days on terrace mats under warm sunlight before slow pounding.

04. Fresh Batches

Hygienic Small-Batch Packing

Ground in small batches of just 15–20kg each week. When your bottle or pack arrives, it smells like a home kitchen that just finished roasted spice pounding.

From Our Terrace & Chekku

How We Make It Every Tuesday & Friday

We started making these powders for our own children and relatives who were tired of commercial spices causing acidity and losing fragrance in two weeks. Today, over 1,400 families across the city rely on our weekly batches.
